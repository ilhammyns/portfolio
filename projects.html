CompTIA Network+ Certification

I completed the CompTIA Network+ certification on February 13, 2024. It covers networking concepts such as network infrastructure, network operations, security, and troubleshooting. I prepared with a video course and virtual labs, and I focused mostly on network security, learning how firewalls work and how to secure a wireless network.

Foundations: Data, Data, Everywhere

I completed this course offered by Google through Coursera, on August 20, 2024. It introduces data analytics and the process analysts follow, from asking questions to sharing results. It also covers what data analysts do and the tools they use, such as spreadsheets and SQL.

Foundations of Cybersecurity

I completed this course offered by Google through Coursera, on August 15, 2024. It introduces the field of cybersecurity and the role of a security analyst. It covers common threats such as phishing and malware and why organizations protect their systems and data.

Student Life Portal Project Proposal (BUSI 3550U)

In a group of five for BUSI 3550U, we wrote a project proposal to modernize Ontario Tech's Student Life Portal focused on its peer tutoring service. We set four objectives: a modern interface, simpler scheduling, and notifications fixes for calendar inconsistencies, and one color-coded calendar with filters backed by a SWOT analysis such as scope, constraints, stakeholders, and a 10-week plan.
